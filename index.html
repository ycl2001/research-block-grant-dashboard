Reference Methodology, sources and limitations View details

Data sources

  • Australian Government Department of Education, Research Block Grant allocations time series.
  • Australian Government Department of Education, Higher Degree by Research student completions time series.

Metric definitions

Total RBG
RSP + RTP
Provider Share
100 × provider Total RBG ÷ annual sector Total RBG
Top-Five Share
Sum of the five highest provider shares in each year
Share Movement
2026 provider share − 2017 provider share, for HEP codes present in both years

Limitations

  • Allocations are formula-driven and funding share is not a measure of research quality or efficiency.
  • Institutions differ in discipline mix and research profile.
  • 2021 included exceptional additional RSP funding associated with COVID-19.
  • The 2026 institutional structure reflects the Adelaide merger; predecessor institutions are not assigned losses.
  • HDR data ends earlier than the RBG allocation series and is presented only as context.

Department of Education public data. Dashboard calculations are reproducible from the source workbooks.
